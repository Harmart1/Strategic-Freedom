ABOUT STRATEGIC FREEDOM

Northern Ontario experience. Straightforward advice.

Strategic Freedom is based in Sault Ste. Marie. Our work brings together business development, governance-related experience, entrepreneurship and economic development. We take on writing, research and strategic assignments where those backgrounds are useful.

How we approach an assignment

We begin with the question people are really asking. Sometimes that means going through a report or a stack of correspondence. Other times it means talking through what a business owner, council or candidate needs to say in public. The aim is to produce work that is accurate, readable and useful on the day it is needed.

Projects may involve a business, a municipality, a community group or a candidate. Before taking anything on, we discuss the work, the deadline, possible conflicts and any rules that apply.
