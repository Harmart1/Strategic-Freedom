Who we are

Strategic Freedom is a consulting business based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario. This Privacy Policy covers personal information collected when you visit this website, send a message, or request email updates. A separate client agreement or privacy notice may apply if we accept a consulting engagement.

For privacy questions or to request access or correction, contact the person responsible for privacy at strategicfreedom@icloud.com.

What information we collect

  • Contact requests: the name, email address, area of interest and message you enter.
  • Email-update requests: an email address, an optional name, your affirmative opt-in, the consent wording and submission timestamp.
  • Technical information: a website host and the external form service may receive ordinary connection and security data, such as IP addresses, browser information and spam-check data. The scope depends on those services.
  • Messages sent directly: the contents and metadata of your email correspondence with us.

We do not ask for payment card details, government identification numbers, or sensitive records through the public website.

Why we use the information

We use contact information to review and respond to inquiries, arrange introductory discussions, manage correspondence and assess potential engagements. We use update signup details to record your request for occasional emails about communications, campaigns, municipal matters, events and business topics. We may also use information as reasonably necessary to address security concerns, meet legal obligations or deal with an access or privacy request.

Submitting an inquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails or create a professional-client relationship.

Email consent and unsubscribing

Signing up for updates is optional and requires a separate, unchecked consent box that you choose to select. We do not treat a website inquiry as consent to receive promotional email. You can withdraw consent by emailing strategicfreedom@icloud.com with “Unsubscribe” in the subject. We will stop commercial messages without delay and no later than 10 business days after receiving your request.

We keep records reasonably needed to show when and how a signup was requested and will remove or suppress addresses when required. Each commercial message is intended to identify the sender and provide a working unsubscribe option, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation.

The website currently forwards signup requests to the inbox for manual list handling; it is not an automated newsletter platform.

Website providers and sharing

The website's forms use FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. Information you submit is sent through FormSubmit and forwarded to the Strategic Freedom iCloud address. Depending on the service's operating practices, FormSubmit may temporarily retain submissions for delivery, spam prevention or recovery. Our email and website hosting providers may process personal information to deliver their services.

We do not sell mailing lists or submitted messages. We may share information with service providers acting on our behalf, with your direction or consent, or where authorized or required by law. Please review the provider's published privacy document for its own practices.

Where information may be processed

We operate in Ontario, but external form, email and hosting services may process or store information in Canada or elsewhere. Information handled in another country may be subject to that country's laws and lawful-access processes. We have not independently verified every provider's storage location, so this policy does not promise Canada-only processing.

Security and how long we keep it

We seek to limit the personal information collected through the site and to use appropriate safeguards for the information under our control. No website submission, email transmission or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send privileged material, passwords, account numbers, medical records or other sensitive information through the public forms.

We keep inquiries for as long as reasonably needed to respond, administer correspondence, meet lawful obligations or address a dispute. Email-update consent records may be kept for as long as needed to show the basis on which commercial messages were sent. Information no longer needed should be securely deleted or de-identified, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. We do not publish a fixed retention period that has not yet been implemented and verified.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, ask questions about our handling of your information, or withdraw consent where permitted. Email strategicfreedom@icloud.com with enough information to identify the relevant communication. We may ask you to verify your identity before disclosing personal information. Applicable law may limit some requests or require us to retain particular records.

If you believe an issue has not been resolved, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Cookies, analytics and external links

The website as currently built does not intentionally run a separate advertising analytics or behaviour-profiling platform. The hosting environment, spam protection and any external service you choose to use may nevertheless use technical logs, cookies or similar technology. Third-party websites linked from this site have their own privacy terms, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, service providers or law changes. A revised version will be posted here with a new date. If a change materially affects a purpose for which consent is required, we will seek additional consent where required rather than relying only on a posted update.
